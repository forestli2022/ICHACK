Reading Prescriptions

Helping educators choose the right book at the right moment for vulnerable and underrepresented youth.

Problem Definition

Charities and educators know that reading can support confidence, identity, and learning. However, book selection is often based only on age or reading level.

This leads to disengagement, wasted time, and missed opportunities— especially for children facing language barriers, low confidence, or difficult life circumstances.

Our Solution

This tool acts as a decision-support system for educators and youth workers. Instead of generic recommendations, it generates needs-based reading prescriptions.

Recommended Books

Book A

Chosen to support confidence through relatable characters and short chapters that reduce cognitive load.

Use: Read-aloud followed by guided discussion.
Book B

Reinforces language skills while addressing themes of belonging without sensitive or triggering content.

Use: Independent reading with weekly check-ins.

Why This Is Feasible & Scalable

  • Built as a lightweight web app — no accounts or complex onboarding
  • Uses a small, curated book dataset suitable for charity contexts
  • AI logic is explainable and constraint-based, not a black box
  • Can be adapted across charities, age groups, and languages

Impact & Storytelling

Before: An educator guesses which book might work, hoping it resonates.

After: The educator selects a book with a clear purpose, aligned to the child’s current needs, and knows what outcome to look for.

This improves engagement, reduces dropout, and helps charities deliver measurable educational and emotional impact.
